Account and Data Deletion Request: Brain Plus Academy

App Name: Brain Plus Academy
Developer: Devansh Gupta

How to Request Account Deletion
To request permanent account deletion and the removal of your personal data, please follow one of these steps:

  1. In-App Request (Recommended): Open the Brain Plus Academy app, navigate to your Profile > Security Settings, and tap "Request Account Deletion". This will send an immediate, verified request to the Administration.

  2. Contact Admin: Request deletion directly from your Institute Administrator. They can permanently remove your account and data instantly using the "Manage Students" or "Manage Teachers" dashboard.

  3. Email Support: Send an email to brainplusacademy54@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request". Please include your registered email address and phone number. We will verify your identity and process the deletion within 72 hours.

Types of Data That Will Be Deleted
When your account is deleted, our system automatically triggers a cleanup process that permanently removes the following data associated with your User ID:

  • Identity & Account: Your login email, password, phone number, name, and profile details stored in our database and authentication system.

  • Academic Records:

    • Students: Class attendance records, test scores, enrolled subjects, and leave applications.

    • Teachers: Class notes uploaded, attendance taken, and leave requests.

  • Financial Records:

    • Students: Fee payment history and uploaded payment proofs.

    • Teachers: Salary history.

  • Device Data: Your registered FCM tokens for push notifications.

  • Files: Any homework attachments or profile images uploaded to our cloud storage.

Data Retention Policy

  • Active Data: Data is deleted immediately from our live systems upon the Administrator's confirmation of the deletion request.

  • Backups: Residual encrypted copies of data may remain in our database backups for up to 30 days before being automatically overwritten.

  • Legal/Financial Retention: Explicitly requested financial transaction records (such as Fee Receipts) may be retained offline by the institute administration strictly for tax and accounting purposes as required by local law.
